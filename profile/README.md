## 🚀 About Us

Envision SAI is a B2B SaaS and asset intelligence company. We transform raw aerial drone thermography into actionable financial intelligence. By leveraging advanced computer vision and geospatial processing, we automate the inspection of utility-scale solar PV farms and critical infrastructure. 

Our mission is to eliminate slow, hazardous manual sweeps and stop silent revenue leakage by pinpointing thermal anomalies and physical defects with exact GPS coordinates.

## 🧠 What We Build

We bridge the gap between complex operational technology (OT) and bottom-line commercial performance. Our core infrastructure includes:

*   **Automated AI Pipelines:** High-throughput computer vision models that scan, detect, and classify physical defects across large-scale assets.
*   **Geospatial Processing Engines:** Systems that translate raw pixels into interactive, map-based defect coordinates.
*   **Cloud-Native SaaS Dashboard:** A centralized portal offering single-pane-of-glass visibility for multi-site portfolio management, built on a scalable microservices architecture.

## ⚡ Core Business Value

Our pay-per-MW SaaS platform empowers infrastructure owners and O&M contractors to:
1.  **Recover Yield:** Instantly identify failing modules to restore lost power generation.
2.  **Slash Costs:** Transition from reactive sweeps to targeted maintenance with exact GPS repair maps.
3.  **Ensure Compliance:** Generate one-click, audit-ready reports for warranty claims and institutional investors.
